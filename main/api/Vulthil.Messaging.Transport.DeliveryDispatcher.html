Class DeliveryDispatcher

Namespace
Vulthil.Messaging.Transport
Assembly
Vulthil.Messaging.dll

Runs one delivered message through its handlers with the delivery rules every transport shares:

  • Handlers run in rounds, one after another in plan order. The first round runs every handler it is given; each later round runs only the handlers that failed in the round before, so a handler that completed never runs again.
  • Every attempt runs in its own service scope, so one handler's scoped state never reaches another's.
  • A failed handler retries under its own policy until its budget runs out or it throws an exception its policy ignores. Then it has failed for good, and its fault is published at once.
  • Handlers retrying in the same round share one wait: the longest back-off any of their policies asks for. The wait runs in-process, unless the port can redeliver later and no retrying policy is in-memory; then the delivery goes back to the broker (see RedeliverLater).
  • A request consumer runs once and answers with its response or an RpcFault; it never retries and never publishes a fault.
  • When CancellationToken ends the delivery, the dispatcher stops without a fault or a reply for the interrupted attempt (see Abandon).
